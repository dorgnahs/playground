About

Ink first, brief second.

I started out painting jazz club signage for a bar that couldn't afford a real designer, and never really left that palette. These days the clients are bigger — magazines, hospitality brands, the odd record label — but the job is the same: make something warm enough that people stop scrolling for a second.

I work mostly in gouache and risograph, then bring it into vector for production. If your brand could use a little more grain and a little less gradient, we'll get on.
